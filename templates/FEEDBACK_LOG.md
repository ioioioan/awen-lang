# Feedback Log

Feedback records production discoveries. It does not change authoritative sources or the Awen contract until evaluated and accepted.

## FB-000 — Short title

- Status: `OPEN`
- Found during: `<task, session or build>`
- Found at revision: `<commit, tag or source-pack identifier>`
- Reported by: `<human, agent or tool>`
- Affected paths: `<paths>`
- Type: `CONTRADICTION | OMISSION | INVALID_ASSUMPTION | VERIFICATION_FAILURE | OPPORTUNITY`
- Severity: `BLOCKING | MATERIAL | MINOR`

### Observation

State what was discovered without silently deciding the answer.

### Evidence

Record reproducible behaviour, relevant declarations or verification output.

### Proposed resolution

Optional proposal. Keep it separate from the observation.

### Evaluation

Compare the item with current scope, authoritative sources and implementation evidence.

### Outcome

- Decision: `ACCEPTED | REJECTED | DEFERRED | NEEDS_CLARIFICATION | RESOLVED`
- Decision record: `<DEC-000 or not required>`
- Resulting revision: `<commit or pending>`
- Verification: `<evidence or pending>`
